# Synthetic data notice

The bundled `synthetic_crop_panel.csv` was generated by `harvestguard_pk.synthetic` using a fixed pseudorandom seed. District identifiers, crop labels, areas, production and yields are simulated solely to test parsing, feature construction, training, evaluation and archive reproducibility.

These rows are **not** official PBS records, farmer reports, survey measurements, remote-sensing observations or estimates of actual Pakistani crop production. Do not merge them into real datasets, use them to support Pakistan-specific empirical claims, or describe them as field observations. For empirical analysis, fetch the PBS-derived source panel using the documented pipeline.
